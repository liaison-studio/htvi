# Head Tracker+Voice+Instructor

**Head tracking for flight simulators with your iPhone — plus voice commands and an instructor, working as one.**

## What it is

Head Tracker+Voice+Instructor turns your iPhone into a head tracker for flight simulators on a Windows PC. You look left, the view in the cockpit turns left. The same program also understands spoken cockpit commands and includes an instructor that guides you through procedures.

| Part | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Head tracking** | The iPhone's front camera (the one used for Face ID) follows your head. Six directions of movement: turn, tilt, roll, lean forward/back, sideways, up/down. No IR clip, no webcam, no extra hardware. If your face is hidden for a few seconds, tracking comes back by itself. |
| **Voice commands** | Say "gear down" — the program presses the right key in the game. English, French, Russian, German, Italian. |
| **Instructor** | Guides you step by step through your aircraft's procedures (engine start, takeoff, landing), speaks the next step aloud, shows the steps on the iPhone, and gives a flight log and review afterwards. |

Usually these are three separate programs that were never designed to work together. Here they are one system: one setup, one window, one iPhone.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11 (no administrator rights needed to install).
- iPhone with Face ID (iPhone X or newer), iOS 16 or later.
- A cable between the iPhone and the PC, or both on the same Wi-Fi network.
- A supported simulator (see below).
