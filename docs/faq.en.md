# FAQ — Head Tracker+Voice+Instructor

🌐 English · [Русский](faq.ru.md) · [Français](faq.fr.md) · [Deutsch](faq.de.md) · [Italiano](faq.it.md)

### Can I have head tracking without TrackIR?
Yes. The program uses your iPhone as the head tracker. You do not need TrackIR, an IR clip or an infrared camera.

### Do I need a webcam?
No. The iPhone's front camera does the tracking.

### Does it work with IL-2 Sturmovik?
Yes. IL-2 Sturmovik: Great Battles has been tested with a real iPhone: the view follows the head in all directions and can be re-centered by voice. The game sees the program as a standard head-tracking device.

### Does it work with Microsoft Flight Simulator (MSFS)?
It is in testing. The instructor has been tested in a 40-minute session in MSFS 2024 with the Cessna 172. Head tracking in MSFS 2024 and 2020 is being checked with a real iPhone; we will mark it as tested only after that.

### Does it work with X-Plane 12?
Not yet. X-Plane 12 is planned.

### Which iPhone do I need?
An iPhone with Face ID — iPhone X or newer — with iOS 16 or later.

### Cable or Wi-Fi?
Both are supported. With a cable, the iPhone also charges during the flight. With Wi-Fi, the iPhone and the PC must be on the same network.

### Where do I put the iPhone?
In front of you, facing your face — for example on a small stand below or beside the monitor.

### What does the instructor do?
It guides you through your aircraft's procedures step by step — engine start, takeoff, landing — and says the next step aloud. You answer by voice ("done", "repeat", "what next"). After the flight you get a flight log and a review.

### In which languages do voice commands work?
English, French, Russian, German and Italian. Choose the same language on the iPhone (**Settings → Speech language**) and in the PC program (**Settings → Voice**).

### Can I use only head tracking?
Yes. Voice commands and the instructor are optional.

### Do I need an account or a subscription?
No account is needed for head tracking, voice commands or the instructor. The price of the app is not decided yet.

### Does the program send my data anywhere?
No. It does not send anything by itself. To report a problem, you save a report file and send it yourself (see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.en.md)).

### Why does Windows warn me when I install?
The program is not yet signed with a publisher certificate, so Windows does not know the publisher. See [Installation, step 2.4](install.en.md#24-run-the-setup--windows-protected-your-pc).

### How do I ask a question or report a bug?
Open an issue: https://github.com/liaison-studio/htvi/issues/new/choose — or write to liaison.studio.contact@gmail.com.
